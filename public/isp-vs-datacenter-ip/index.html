How to Verify True Static Residential ISP Proxies vs Datacenter IPs

Many proxy vendors market cheap VPS ranges as "residential" or "native US IPs," yet AI platforms and Stripe Radar immediately classify them as hosting traffic. The difference comes down to ASN ownership and IP intelligence registries. A true Static Residential ISP IP is announced under a consumer or business internet service provider ASN (such as Comcast, AT&T, Verizon, Cox, or RCN) and is tagged as isp or business with is_datacenter: false, is_vpn: false, and is_proxy: false. By contrast, datacenter IPs belong to cloud and colocation networks—AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Hetzner, OVH, Zenlayer, or Tencent Cloud—where automated bots and multi-account farms generate persistent abuse signals.

When you run a scan on envguard.dev, the backend combines real-time Cloudflare edge ASN metadata with exact-IP reputation lookups from ipapi.is. Instead of guessing from neighboring IP subnets, each exact exit IP is checked individually for datacenter flags, VPN/proxy markers, company type (isp, business, or hosting), and ASN identity. If your proxy shows Type: Hosting or flags as a proxy/VPN, no amount of cookie clearing will make that IP look residential to Anthropic or Stripe.
